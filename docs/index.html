Six Essential Questions

What · Why · Who · Where · When · How

01
What?
A Collective Intelligence Engine for AI
The Meta-Innovation Harness is a structured Claude Code skill-set — six "Innovation Harness" workflows, a meta-skill (auto-selects the right workflow), and a meta-meta-skill (generates & self-improves harnesses) — that directs four browser-driven frontier LLMs as a coordinated expert ensemble. The worked example targets six open research questions from a Google DeepMind/Gemini leadership talk.
02
Why?
Because No Single Mind Is Enough
Real breakthroughs emerge at disciplinary intersections — biology informing physics, philosophy correcting engineering. The Transactive Systems Model of Collective Intelligence (TSM-CI) and "Group Genius" theory show that structured cross-pollination among diverse experts outperforms any individual, including the best individual AI. This harness operationalises that theory as a repeatable, automatable protocol.
03
Who?
Orchestrator + Four Polymath Super-Experts
An Orchestrator AI (Claude Code, operating as a Triple-PhD MIT Genius) conducts the session. The four frontier LLMs — Claude, ChatGPT/GPT, Gemini, and Grok — each embody a permanent disciplinary stance. Intended users: researchers, strategists, innovation leads, and AI-augmented knowledge workers who need structured ensemble cognition.
04
Where?
Claude Code + Browser via Claude-in-Chrome
Runs entirely inside Claude Code CLI. All four frontier LLMs are driven through the Claude-in-Chrome browser extension — no APIs, no rate limits, no vendor lock-in beyond the four public chat interfaces. Outputs are archived as Markdown under examples/ in the repo for full transparency and reproducibility.
05
When?
Any High-Stakes Research Decision
Deploy when a question is too multi-dimensional for a single expert: frontier science, product strategy, policy, speculative engineering, or moonshot ideation. The inner/outer loop structure scales from a 30-minute "flash team" sprint to a quarterly deep-dive review lasting hours — chosen by the meta-skill based on goal complexity and time budget.
06
How?
Blanking + Leaking Cross-Pollination
For each focal question: blank (delete the prior answer for that question — forcing a clean-slate re-derivation) but leak (show the evolving best answers for all OTHER questions). Rotate across questions in inner loops, then synthesise in outer loops. This destroys anchoring bias while preserving cross-domain signal, producing genuinely novel combinatorial insight.

Every session begins with a persona declaration: the Orchestrator operates as a Triple-PhD MIT Genius — elite of the elite, best on Earth in thought leadership, novelty, creativity, and extreme polymathy, drawing wisdom from ALL fields simultaneously. Six Workflows + Two Meta-Skills

The Innovation Harness Suite

Each workflow is a distinct orchestration protocol — different cadence, format, and cognitive pressure profile. The meta-skill auto-selects; the meta-meta-skill generates new ones.

System Architecture

Inner loops cycle across individual questions; outer loops synthesise across all experts and all questions. The Orchestrator AI holds the state machine.

Theoretical Foundation

Why Blanking + Leaking Works

The method is grounded in cognitive science, collective intelligence theory, and information-theoretic principles of creative recombination.

Group Genius
Sawyer's Collaborative Emergence
Keith Sawyer's research shows that genuine creativity emerges from structured improvisation between diverse experts — not from individuals working alone. The harness encodes this as an algorithmic protocol.
TSM-CI
Transactive Systems Model
The Transactive Memory / Systems Model of Collective Intelligence holds that group IQ exceeds individual IQ when members hold complementary knowledge and communicate it efficiently. The four fixed lenses simulate this maximally.
De-Anchoring
Blanking Destroys Anchoring Bias
Cognitive anchoring — over-relying on the first answer — is the dominant failure mode of sequential LLM reasoning. Forcing deletion of the prior answer and mandatory re-derivation from scratch eliminates this bias completely.
Cross-Signal
Leaking Preserves Combinatorial Signal
While blanking eliminates anchoring within a question, leaking provides the cross-domain signal that drives genuine novelty. Seeing biology's answer before re-deriving the physics answer forces unexpected synthesis.
Delphi
Structured Iteration Toward Truth
Outer loops mirror the Delphi method's multi-round expert elicitation: each pass allows experts to update positions in light of synthesised group output, converging toward robustly defended consensus positions or surfacing genuinely irreducible tensions.
