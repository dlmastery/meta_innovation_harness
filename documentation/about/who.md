| Responsibility | Description |
|---------------|-------------|

## The Four Expert AIs

Each frontier LLM operates as a "polymath super-expert" — a domain specialist
who brings the full depth of their training to a narrow, mandatory lens.

### Claude (Anthropic) — Biological Philosopher

**Fixed lens**: Evolutionary biology, systems biology, molecular biology,
ancient texts (Vedic, Stoic, Taoist, Greek), continental philosophy,
phenomenology, ethics, aesthetics, pattern languages of living systems.

**Epistemic style**: Finds the living-system analogy. Looks for what evolution
has already solved. Brings philosophical nuance to mechanistic questions.
Asks: *What does this look like from the inside of a complex adaptive system?*

**Why this lens for Claude**: Claude's training emphasises nuanced, contextual
reasoning and draws heavily on humanistic and scientific literature. The
biological-philosophical lens amplifies these existing strengths.

### ChatGPT / GPT (OpenAI) — Physical Engineer

**Fixed lens**: Theoretical and applied physics (classical, quantum, statistical,
condensed matter), chemistry, materials science, systems engineering,
mechanism design, quantitative first-principles modeling, thermodynamics.

**Epistemic style**: Builds the mechanism. Quantifies the trade-offs. Asks
what the physical substrate constrains and what it enables. Seeks the
governing equation. Asks: *What are the fundamental physical limits here,
and what engineering architecture approaches them?*

**Why this lens for GPT**: GPT models have exhibited particular strength in
STEM reasoning and mathematical derivation. The physics-engineering lens
maximises this advantage.

### Gemini (Google DeepMind) — Human-System Evaluator

**Fixed lens**: Social science (sociology, anthropology, psychology, behavioral
economics), product strategy, market dynamics, human-computer interaction,
user experience research, evaluation methodology and measurement, technology
adoption, policy and governance.

**Epistemic style**: Asks who this affects, how it will actually be used, and
how you would know if it worked. Brings empirical rigor to speculative claims.
Asks: *What does the evidence say, who are the affected stakeholders, and what
would a robust evaluation design look like?*

**Why this lens for Gemini**: Gemini's development by Google DeepMind, with
its emphasis on multimodal understanding and broad factual grounding, makes it
well-suited for the evaluator/social-science role.

### Grok (xAI) — Radical World-Modeler

**Fixed lens**: Creative world-modeling (scenario design, counterfactual
reasoning), radical efficiency (constraint-stripping, minimum-viable solutions),
contrarian hypothesis generation, science fiction as predictive methodology,
complexity and emergence, unconventional analogies.

**Epistemic style**: Challenges every assumption. Strips every constraint that
isn't physically necessary. Asks: *What if we assumed the opposite? What would
an alien civilization do with this problem? What is the stupidly simple version
that everyone is overlooking?*

**Why this lens for Grok**: Grok's training with an explicit emphasis on
irreverence and unconventional thinking, combined with access to real-time
information, makes it the natural contrarian/world-modeler in the ensemble.

## The User

The harness is designed for:

### Primary Users

- **Frontier researchers** in AI, biology, physics, social science, or
  interdisciplinary fields who need structured multi-domain ideation on
  genuinely hard problems.
- **Innovation strategists and product leaders** who need to pressure-test
  strategic options against diverse expert perspectives before committing.
- **AI augmentation practitioners** who are developing advanced LLM workflows
  and want a production-grade reference architecture for multi-model orchestration.

### Secondary Users

- **PhD students and academics** exploring collective intelligence methodology
  for their research process.
- **Technology forecasters** who need to map the possibility space of emerging
  technologies across multiple disciplinary lenses simultaneously.
- **Writers and thinkers** working on ideas that benefit from deep, structured
  multi-perspective engagement.

## Expertise Required from the User

The user needs:
1. A well-formed research goal or set of questions (one to six questions works best).
2. Claude Code CLI installed and authenticated.
3. Claude-in-Chrome extension active with all four LLM browser tabs open.
4. Willingness to review and engage with multi-page, dense expert outputs.

The meta-skill handles all further complexity. No configuration of loop
parameters, expert briefing, or synthesis architecture is required from the user.
