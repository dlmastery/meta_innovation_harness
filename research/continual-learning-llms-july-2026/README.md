## Central conclusion

Continual learning is first a measurement and governed-state problem, then a falsifiable substrate-placement problem, and only then an optimization problem. The reviewed public evidence does not demonstrate an indefinitely online weight-updating LLM that simultaneously guarantees retention, plasticity, bounded resources, provenance, deletion, rollback, and safety.

## Independence limitation

The five contenders used overlapping controller context and peer-derived material. Their agreement reflects synthesis consistency, not independent empirical replication.
