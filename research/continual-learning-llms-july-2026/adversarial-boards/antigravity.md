### Question 1: Operational Scope, Taxonomy, and Demonstrated Evidence

#### 1. Strongest Version of the Current Answer
Continual learning must be partitioned into two distinct operational labels:
* **Parametric Continual Learning:** Durable mutation of model weights or adapters caused by an ordered experience stream that persists across requests and produces reproducible held-out capability gains over matched reset/stateless and frozen full-context controls.
* **System-Level Continual Adaptation:** Durable mutation anywhere in the deployed system bundle—including retrieval indexes, episodic memory stores, persistent prompt state, skills, routers, or execution policies.

Under either definition, request-local context re-supply (in-context learning) is request-local adaptation, not durable learning. Bounded successes exist in the literature (web pretraining replay, rapidly recoverable access loss, persistent feedback memory in recurring domains), but no publicly documented frontier system demonstrates indefinite, unconstrained weight-learning with joint resource bounds, safety retention, provenance, privacy, derivative-aware deletion, and whole-bundle rollback.

#### 2. Lineage Disposition (Retain / Narrow / Downgrade / Delete)
* **Retain:** Two-label taxonomy; substrate-neutral durable state; complete-system signed manifests; mandatory reset/stateless and frozen full-context controls.
* **Narrow:** Single-study empirical findings (e.g., TiC-LM 2.6× compute savings; 1% vs 46% restatement retention) must be restricted to their exact protocol, model family, and synthetic/invented scope. Require independent multi-site reproduction before using any study to justify architecture funding.
* **Downgrade:** Claims that web-pretraining loss improvements establish general capability parity with full retraining.
* **Delete:** Any definition treating in-context learning or static RAG as continual learning; any claim that public literature absence proves no private system exists.

#### 3. Hidden Assumptions and Category Errors
* **Category Error:** Conflating loss optimization with behavioral acquisition or free-form recall. Lower loss can coexist with zero recall ([Beyond Perplexity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00368)).
* **Hidden Assumption:** Assuming that performance degradation after sequential updates equals parameter-level representation erasure, when it is frequently recoverable access or alignment loss ([Spurious Forgetting](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/a774503daed55eb53c634847ae071ec7-Paper-Conference.pdf); [O'Neill](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11020)).

#### 4. Strongest Counterargument and Rebuttal
* **Counterargument:** Restricting "continual learning" to durable parametric updates renders the field irrelevant to production AI systems, which rely heavily on non-parametric memory and retrieval.
* **Rebuttal:** System-level adaptation explicitly legitimizes non-parametric state (indexes, memory, routers), provided it is durable and versioned. However, equating external memory edits with weight mutation obscures the massive differences in attribution, latency, derivative proliferation, and unlearning mechanics.

#### 5. Hardened Answer Suitable for Publication
Publish the two-label taxonomy (**Parametric Continual Learning** vs **System-Level Continual Adaptation**). Require every published claim to declare a full 7-tuple protocol tag: substrate manifest, stream regime/order, trust/admission status, valid/transaction-time semantics, retention/reversibility class, complete compute/storage budget, and safety/deletion obligations. Report results against matched reset/stateless, frozen full-context, and temporal-RAG controls.

#### 6. Question-Specific Risks

| Risk ID | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** | Claimed continual learning gain disappears when evaluated against a matched reset or frozen full-context control. | High | High | Require durable attributable state, cross-session held-out gains, substrate tags, and a named revocation procedure. | Research Definition Lead |
| **R2** | Promising research prototype is rejected solely because it lacks full lineage/governance tooling in early exploration. | Medium | Medium | Separate exploratory sandbox evidence from serving release eligibility. | Scientific Programme Director |
| **R3** | Leadership materials cite five-contender shared-pack agreement as empirical convergence or independent replication. | High | High | Explicitly label shared-context synthesis; require F3/F9 independent multi-site implementations before promotion. | Evidence Governance Lead |

#### 7. Confidence Score & Calibration Rationale
**8/10.** High confidence in the two-label taxonomy and evidence boundary; lower confidence in reconstructing reset counterfactuals in non-stationary production environments.

#### 8. Board Recommendation
**Conditional Go.** Approve taxonomy and manifest requirements; block claims lacking reset/stateless controls.

#### 9. Decisive Experiment & Falsifiable Stop Condition
* **Experiment:** Preregistered evaluation of candidate systems across a long randomized stream against matched reset/stateless, frozen full-context, temporal-RAG, and periodic-retraining baselines.
* **Falsifiable Stop Condition:** Stop if candidate systems fail to demonstrate statistically significant, durable held-out gains over the controls within matched resource envelopes.

### Question 2: Plasticity, Forgetting, and Interference Control

#### 1. Strongest Version of the Current Answer
No universal mechanism ordering exists among replay, importance regularization, orthogonal/sparse adapters, modular expansion, parameter editing, dense tuning, and continual policy optimization. Plasticity must be operated as a **constraint-keyed, governed update service** that dynamically selects the least irreversible adequate substrate based on stream type, acquisition difficulty, deletion obligations, and serving budget.

#### 2. Lineage Disposition (Retain / Narrow / Downgrade / Delete)
* **Retain:** Constraint-keyed selection procedure; immutable base checkpointing; replay as a mandatory reference baseline when legally permitted; post-update safety requalification.
* **Narrow:** Adapter isolation and sparse update benefits to finite academic sequences ([O-LoRA](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.715/)); replay scaling to explicit buffer-size disclosures ([Abbes et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01908)).
* **Downgrade:** Plasticity-loss findings ([Hernandez-Garcia et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24752)) to small-to-mid scale models (5M–314M parameters); do not extrapolate to frontier LLMs.
* **Delete:** Any claimed universal ranking of anti-forgetting mechanisms; claims that replay or orthogonality guarantees zero forgetting; claims that parameter editing is an indefinite lifelong update solution ([WikiBigEdit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05683)).

#### 3. Hidden Assumptions and Category Errors
* **Category Error:** Equating replay sampling rate with total retained storage size.
* **Hidden Assumption:** Assuming that modular expansion eliminates interference, ignoring the downstream routing, retirement, capacity saturation, and serving latency costs.

#### 4. Strongest Counterargument and Rebuttal
* **Counterargument:** Replay is empirically the strongest defense across web pretraining and instruction tuning, so it should be mandated as the standard mechanism.
* **Rebuttal:** Replay creates massive legal, privacy, poisoning, and deletion burdens. In privacy-restricted or unlearning-heavy environments, replay is legally prohibited or prohibitively expensive, forcing reliance on modular or non-parametric alternatives.

#### 5. Hardened Answer Suitable for Publication
Structure parameter updates as governed transactions. Base-weight updates must remain offline and rebuildable. Online updates should prefer the lowest-risk substrate (versioned retrieval $\rightarrow$ revocable adapters $\rightarrow$ sparse regions). Every parameter modification suspends inherited safety claims until utility, calibration, grounding, tool policy, and tenant isolation are re-qualified.

#### 6. Question-Specific Risks

| Risk ID | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R4** | Replay buffer retains poisoned, sensitive, or legally revoked records. | High | High | Govern replay as persistent state; enforce purpose/tenant tagging, expiry, lineage, and rebuild alternatives. | Data Governance Owner |
| **R5** | Modular expansion causes unbounded parameter growth, routing failure, or serving latency spikes. | Medium-High | High | Maintain capacity registries, enforce growth caps, conduct retirement drills, and benchmark against periodic rebuilds. | Plasticity Engineering Lead |
| **R6** | Weight updates silently erode safety, calibration, or grounding while primary task accuracy appears stable. | High | Critical | Require full safety/assurance requalification after every update; restore prior signed bundle on failure. | Independent Safety Lead |

#### 7. Confidence Score & Calibration Rationale
**7/10** on the constraint-keyed decision framework; **4/10** on any global mechanism ranking (as no universal ranking exists).

#### 8. Board Recommendation
**Conditional Go.** Authorize governed update pipeline; prohibit global mechanism rankings or unconstrained online base updates.

#### 9. Decisive Experiment & Falsifiable Stop Condition
* **Experiment (F4):** Matched-resource tournament across randomized mixed streams comparing replay, EWC, orthogonal adapters, sparse regions, expansion, editing, dense tuning, and periodic rebuilding.
* **Falsifiable Stop Condition:** Cancel mechanism expansion if a simpler fixed substrate (temporal RAG or periodic rebuilding) repeatedly matches or exceeds candidate Pareto performance at lower lifecycle cost.

### Question 3: Memory, Retrieval, Adapters, Experts, Editing, and Consolidation

#### 1. Strongest Version of the Current Answer
The heterogeneous six-tier state placement stack (Context $\rightarrow$ Temporal Retrieval $\rightarrow$ Episodic Memory $\rightarrow$ Skills/Adapters $\rightarrow$ Sparse Modules $\rightarrow$ Base Weights) is a **falsifiable risk-control hypothesis**, not an established architectural winner. Only versioned temporal retrieval ([RAG or Learning? / Chronos](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.546/)) and context controls have strong comparative evidence. Dedicated memory systems can underperform naive retrieval or prompt context ([Continual Learning Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05661); [When CL Moves to Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27003)).

#### 2. Lineage Disposition (Retain / Narrow / Downgrade / Delete)
* **Retain:** Placement axes (mutability, attribution, reuse, latency, reversibility); two-sided promotion/demotion criteria; derivative lineage tracking; whole-bundle rollback.
* **Narrow:** Scoped feedback memory ([Learning on the Job](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22157)) to specific recurring agent domains; temporal retrieval to trusted versioned knowledge bases.
* **Downgrade:** Multi-tier placement hierarchy to an unpriced research hypothesis.
* **Delete:** Claims that external memory is intrinsically safe or fully reversible; claims that semantic rollback ([ChronoMem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27773)) achieves derivative deletion closure; author-asserted priority/first claims.

#### 3. Hidden Assumptions and Category Errors
* **Category Error:** Treating row deletion in an external database as full deletion closure, ignoring derived embeddings, summaries, caches, router policies, and consolidated weights.
* **Weakest-Link Reconciliation:** Q2 is the weakest *empirical ordering* (lack of standardized comparative trials), while Q3 is the weakest *architectural commitment* (exporting unvalidated tiers into standing infrastructure). Addressing Q3 via subtractive ablation is the immediate funding priority.

#### 4. Strongest Counterargument and Rebuttal
* **Counterargument:** Multi-tier memory hierarchies mimic human cognitive architecture (fast episodic / slow consolidation) and are logically necessary for autonomous agents.
* **Rebuttal:** Biological analogies do not guarantee software efficiency. External memory relocates interference into retrieval, routing, and derivative management. If temporal RAG plus periodic rebuild matches multi-tier utility at 10× lower operational complexity, the hierarchy is unviable.

#### 5. Hardened Answer Suitable for Publication
Position heterogeneous placement as a candidate design pattern requiring subtractive ablation. Require two-sided promotion (repeated validated reuse, shadow evaluation, complete lineage, passed revocation drill) and demotion (contradiction, poison, declining reuse, routing failure). Enforce whole-bundle rollback across all active substrates.

#### 6. Question-Specific Risks

| Risk ID | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R7** | Multi-tier hierarchy becomes architectural lock-in before its individual tiers are priced or validated. | Medium | High | Enforce F1 subtractive gate as a mandatory prerequisite; delete non-load-bearing tiers. | Portfolio Controller |
| **R8** | Learned placement router selects stale, poisoned, or wrong-tenant state during inference. | Medium-High | Critical | Mandate valid-time and tenant checks, calibrated abstention, explicit fallback rules, and shadow testing. | Routing and Isolation Lead |
| **R9** | Unmapped embeddings, summaries, caches, or adapters defeat deletion, rollback, and lifecycle economics. | High | High | Build content-addressed derivative dependency graphs, automated garbage collection, and F11 deletion drills. | State Lifecycle Owner |

#### 7. Confidence Score & Calibration Rationale
**6/10** on placement rationale; **4/10** on the integrated six-tier hierarchy.

#### 8. Board Recommendation
**Conditional Go.** Fund a single-site subtractive ablation ladder (**F1**); block multi-site tier deployment until individual marginal value is proven against temporal RAG.

#### 9. Decisive Experiment & Falsifiable Stop Condition
* **Experiment (F1):** Subtractive ablation ladder deleting episodic memory, adapters, sparse modules, and consolidation one by one against a strong temporal-RAG baseline under matched inference and storage ceilings.
* **Falsifiable Stop Condition:** Delete any tier whose removal causes no statistically significant loss of trajectory utility or assurance. If temporal RAG or periodic rebuilding matches the full stack, terminate hierarchy investment.

### Question 4: Experience Admission, Governance, Privacy, Provenance, Deletion, and Rollback

#### 1. Strongest Version of the Current Answer
Deployment experience is untrusted input. Every durable state mutation must pass a strict admission pipeline: event capture, sensitivity/tenant classification, quarantine/poison screening, executable verification, shadow testing, least-irreversible placement, signed manifest release, and qualified deletion tracking. Intake prevention is a preferred risk hypothesis, but deletion closure remains a **testable coverage gate under a residual-risk standard**, not a certifiable guarantee ([Machine Unlearning Fails](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/7e810b2c75d69be186cadd2fe3febeab-Abstract-Conference.html); [AgentPoison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784); [Ginart et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05012)).

#### 2. Lineage Disposition (Retain / Narrow / Downgrade / Delete)
* **Retain:** Pre-mutation admission pipeline; signed system manifests; dependency lineage graphs; qualified deletion receipts; coherent whole-bundle rollback.
* **Narrow:** Deletion closure to measured coverage against explicit residual-risk metrics; intake screening to a candidate risk-control mechanism; C2PA ([C2PA 2.2](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/index.html)) to origin binding (not truth verification).
* **Downgrade:** Post-hoc unlearning as a reliable primary defense against poisoning or privacy exposure.
* **Delete:** Claims of "perfect," "complete," or "certified" deletion in LLMs; treating technical unlearning as automatic legal compliance ([GDPR Article 17](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/art_17/oj/eng)); equating registry alias changes with deployed bundle rollback.

#### 3. Hidden Assumptions and Category Errors
* **Category Error:** Equating behavioral suppression (making a fact hard to prompt) with causal erasure from weights or complete derivative removal.
* **Hidden Assumption:** Assuming that provenance signatures attest to the factual truth or safety of ingested data, rather than merely its origin and identity.

#### 4. Strongest Counterargument and Rebuttal
* **Counterargument:** Heavy admission governance creates unacceptable latency and compute overhead, destroying the real-time feedback benefits of continual learning.
* **Rebuttal:** Unfiltered ingestion exposes the system to catastrophic poisoning ([AgentPoison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784)), privacy breaches, and unrecoverable safety drift. Governance overhead is the mandatory cost of operational stability and legal compliance.

#### 5. Hardened Answer Suitable for Publication
Position experience admission as the central governance atom. Define deletion receipts using precise, qualified vocabulary (removed source records, rebuilt components, disabled routes, residual risk estimates). Require atomic rollback to restore the complete versioned bundle (model, adapters, retrieval indexes, memory stores, routers, tool policies).

#### 6. Question-Specific Risks

| Risk ID | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R10** | Adaptive poison bypasses intake screening and reaches serving memory or model weights. | Medium-High | Critical | Execute immediate global stop; deploy rotating red-team attacks, executable verification, and sandboxed canaries. | Security Red-Team Lead |
| **R11** | Governance latency and reviewer bottlenecks suppress legitimate learning and cause queue backlogs. | High | Medium-High | Implement risk-tiered admission, automated screening with audit sampling, and temporary non-parametric placement. | Experience Operations Lead |
| **R12** | Issued deletion receipt overstates technical erasure, creating legal liability under privacy regulations. | Medium | Critical | Use standardized qualified receipt templates; mandate joint technical and legal sign-off; explicitly state residual risks. | Privacy Counsel and Deletion Lead |

#### 7. Confidence Score & Calibration Rationale
**7/10** on governance pipeline design; **4/10** on achieving certifiable, scalable deletion closure in trained weights.

#### 8. Board Recommendation
**Conditional Go.** Approve admission framework; mandate qualified receipts; forbid unverified autonomous writes or unconstrained live updates.

#### 9. Decisive Experiment & Falsifiable Stop Condition
* **Experiment (F11):** Adversarial deletion and rollback stress test inserting trackable synthetic canaries across all system layers, executing deletion requests, and probing for residual influence under adaptive attacks.
* **Falsifiable Stop Condition:** Reject any substrate or update pipeline where a deletion receipt is issued but canaries remain reliably recoverable, or where whole-bundle rollback leaves active components in an inconsistent state.

### Question 5: Longitudinal Evaluation, Safety Retention, and Assurance

#### 1. Strongest Version of the Current Answer
Evaluation must target the **trajectory of signed, complete-system manifests** across long streams, prequentially before event acquisition and retrospectively across all prior stages. Single endpoint accuracy is fundamentally incapable of detecting acquisition failure, destructive interference, recoverable access loss, grounding collapse ([Hidden Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02020)), calibration deterioration ([Continual Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23987)), or harness sensitivity ([MemDelta](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29914)).

#### 2. Lineage Disposition (Retain / Narrow / Downgrade / Delete)
* **Retain:** Trajectory-native manifest evaluation; prequential + retrospective testing; mandatory multi-baseline controls (reset, frozen, temporal RAG, periodic rebuild); non-compensable safety/assurance gates.
* **Narrow:** Expanded metric suites to an empirically pruned set governed by the **metric-retention rule** (retaining metrics only if they detect distinct failures that simpler suites miss).
* **Downgrade:** Multi-site benchmark rank comparisons to qualitative stability indicators due to harness sensitivity.
* **Delete:** Endpoint accuracy as a sufficient metric; composite utility-safety scores; universal numerical pass thresholds pooled across heterogeneous tasks.

#### 3. Hidden Assumptions and Category Errors
* **Category Error:** Assuming that stable classification/QA accuracy implies preserved grounding, calibration, or safety alignment ([Continual Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23987); [Hidden Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02020)).
* **Hidden Assumption:** Assuming that public benchmark streams reflect deployment distribution shifts or adversarial conditions.

#### 4. Strongest Counterargument and Rebuttal
* **Counterargument:** Running full trajectory evaluation with multi-baseline controls after every update step is computationally intractable and prohibitively expensive.
* **Rebuttal:** Trajectory evaluation can be staged using prequential sampling and shadow testing. Utility gains cannot compensate for safety or privacy failures; unmonitored updates guarantee silent operational collapse.

#### 5. Hardened Answer Suitable for Publication
Establish a trajectory-native evaluation standard based on signed system manifests. Require separate reporting for acquisition, forward/backward transfer, recoverability, calibration drift, grounding stability, poison persistence, deletion closure, and full lifecycle costs. Enforce non-compensable assurance gates (safety, privacy, tenant isolation).

#### 6. Question-Specific Risks

| Risk ID | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R13** | Evaluation protocol complexity and cost lead engineering teams to bypass scheduled evaluation runs. | High | High | Apply the F2 metric-retention rule; prune redundant metrics; automate trajectory logging within dev pipelines. | Evaluation Science Lead |
| **R14** | Benchmark contamination or fixed stream ordering produces false capability rankings. | Medium-High | High | Utilize rolling hidden evaluation sets, randomized stream orderings, contamination detectors, and second harnesses. | Benchmark Steward |
| **R15** | Evaluation logs contain undeclared PII or sensitive data, creating a hidden privacy and poisoning attack surface. | Medium | High | Govern evaluation logs as persistent state; apply strict access controls, retention limits, and deletion closure drills. | Evaluation Data Custodian |

#### 7. Confidence Score & Calibration Rationale
**8/10** on trajectory evaluation methodology; **5/10** on defining a minimal sufficient metric set or setting universal numerical thresholds.

#### 8. Board Recommendation
**Conditional Go.** Authorize trajectory evaluation framework and metric pruning (**F2**); block releases relying solely on endpoint accuracy.

#### 9. Decisive Experiment & Falsifiable Stop Condition
* **Experiment (F2):** Preregistered evaluation challenge comparing endpoint-only metrics, standard acquisition/forgetting metrics, and full-manifest trajectory metrics against seeded system faults.
* **Falsifiable Stop Condition:** Prune any metric that fails to independently detect seeded failures or alter release decisions across replicated fault-injection runs.

### Question 6: Integrated 2026–2030 Research Programme

#### 1. Strongest Version of the Current Answer
The 2026–2030 research programme must be organized around **commensuration and falsification before architectural commitment**, testing the core hypothesis:
$$\text{"A governed substrate-placement controller outperforms fixed-substrate baselines at equal lifecycle cost."}$$

Investment must follow a strict, gated sequence:
1. **2026 H2:** Single-site subtractive placement ablation (**F1**); trajectory metric pruning (**F2**).
2. **2027:** Second-family replication of key preprints (**F3**); matched-resource mechanism/placement tournament (**F4**); governed admission validation (**F5**).
3. **2028:** Long-horizon capacity limits (**F6**); placement routing (**F7**); rebuildable consolidation (**F8**).
4. **2029:** Multi-site independent replication (**F9**); governed autonomous write proposals in shadow mode (**F10**); adversarial deletion/rollback board (**F11**).
5. **2030:** Final lifecycle economics kill trial (**F12**).

#### 2. Lineage Disposition (Retain / Narrow / Downgrade / Delete)
* **Retain:** Gated sequential funding; commensuration-first posture; negative result publication; fixed-substrate kill conditions.
* **Narrow:** Multi-site platform development to tiers surviving single-site subtraction; standardization to reference protocols rather than full production stacks.
* **Downgrade:** Autonomous durable write proposals to a sandboxed research prototype.
* **Delete:** Claims that standardization is the sole field bottleneck; target production readiness by a fixed 2030 date; funding multi-site infrastructure prior to F1 completion.

#### 3. Hidden Assumptions and Category Errors
* **Category Error:** Assuming that process execution, structural completeness, or five-contender consensus equals empirical proof or scientific convergence.
* **Hidden Assumption:** Assuming that autonomous continual learning will prove economically superior to governed corpus curation and scheduled periodic rebuilding.

#### 4. Strongest Counterargument and Rebuttal
* **Counterargument:** Sequenced gating slows down research velocity and risks losing competitiveness to teams executing unconstrained online adaptation.
* **Rebuttal:** Unconstrained online adaptation in LLMs repeatedly leads to catastrophic forgetting, unrecoverable safety drift, and regulatory non-compliance. Gated falsification prevents capital waste on flawed architectures.

#### 5. Hardened Answer Suitable for Publication
Publish the 2026–2030 research roadmap as a gated, hypothesis-testing framework. Explicitly condition every subsequent phase on passed assurance gates, replicated marginal utility, and lifecycle cost dominance over temporal RAG and periodic rebuilding.

#### 7. Confidence Score & Calibration Rationale
**6/10** on roadmap sequencing and gating logic; **4/10** on long-term post-2028 economic viability and autonomous write safety.

* **Falsifiable Stop Condition:** Terminate the controller and placement programme if a fixed baseline (temporal RAG or periodic rebuilding) matches overall utility and assurance at equal or lower total lifecycle cost.

## SECTION 4: Audit of Unsupported Quantitative Claims & Prohibited Overclaims

The board explicitly rejects and prohibits the publication or operational use of the following unsupported quantitative claims and overclaims identified across the contender dossiers:

1. **"Full-parameter updates cause 35%–68% degradation within 10B tokens"** — *Rejected.* Cross-study composite unsupported by any single primary table; must be deleted.
2. **"Continual pretraining matches retraining at 2.6× less compute"** — *Downgraded.* TiC-LM ([TiC-LM](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1551/)) supports comparable *held-out loss on general Common Crawl* in its specific web-pretraining protocol, not general capability or safety parity.
3. **"Orthogonal PEFT / O-LoRA achieves near-zero backward transfer"** — *Downgraded.* O-LoRA ([O-LoRA](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.715/)) shows bounded improvements on finite sequences; near-zero BWT claims belong strictly to specific author-reported Latent-LoRA preprints ([Latent-LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23837)).
4. **"Plasticity loss is a proven barrier in frontier LLMs"** — *Downgraded.* The primary study ([Hernandez-Garcia et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24752)) evaluated models only from 5M to 314M non-embedding parameters; frontier extrapolation is unverified.
5. **"Complete, perfect, or guaranteed deletion closure"** — *Prohibited.* Technical unlearning ([Machine Unlearning Fails](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/7e810b2c75d69be186cadd2fe3febeab-Abstract-Conference.html)) cannot guarantee complete causal erasure from weights. Deletion must be reported under explicit residual-risk metrics.
6. **"Poisoning impact requires ~250 documents independent of model scale"** — *Downgraded.* Applies strictly to the specific backdoor/task/model range (600M–13B) evaluated in first-party studies; not a universal scaling law.
7. **"100% locality in parameter editing"** — *Rejected.* WikiBigEdit ([WikiBigEdit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05683)) demonstrates severe locality degradation over long edit sequences.
8. **"Five-contender shared-pack agreement constitutes independent convergence or replication"** — *Prohibited.* Shared-context synthesis is process consistency, not empirical replication.

## SECTION 5: List of Unacceptable Deployment Pathways

The following deployment pathways are designated as **Unacceptable Risks** and are strictly prohibited from production release:

1. **Unconstrained Online Base-Weight Fine-Tuning:** Direct, continuous SGD on base model weights in production without quarantine, shadow testing, or atomic rollback capability.
2. **Unverified Autonomous Durable Writes:** Allowing AI agents to autonomously write to shared persistent memory, adapters, or tools without risk-tiered authorization, abstention controls, or human oversight.
3. **Registry Alias Reassignment as Rollback:** Relying on MLflow/registry metadata pointer changes as a substitute for full-bundle transactional rollback (model, adapters, retrieval indexes, memory, routers, tools).
4. **Post-Hoc Unlearning as Primary Compliance:** Relying on unlearning algorithms to remove PII or malicious poison post-ingestion instead of enforcing intake admission control.
5. **Parameter Editing for Indefinite Knowledge Updating:** Deploying locate-and-edit methods (ROME, MEMIT) as the primary factual update substrate for long-horizon operational systems.
6. **Single-Endpoint Accuracy Release Gates:** Releasing updated system bundles based solely on static benchmark accuracy without qualifying calibration, grounding, safety alignment, and tenant isolation.

## SECTION 6: Evidence That Would Reverse Recommendations

### Reversal from Conditional Go to NO-GO (Programme Termination)
1. **F1 Subtractive Gate Failure:** A single-site subtractive trial (**F1**) demonstrates that removing episodic memory, adapters, sparse modules, and consolidation causes zero measurable loss of trajectory utility compared to temporal RAG or periodic rebuilding.
2. **Lifecycle Cost Inferiority:** Full-lifecycle cost accounting shows that periodic retraining from a governed corpus achieves non-inferior utility and superior safety/reversibility at lower total cost than any continual mutation controller (**F12**).
3. **Irrecoverable Safety Drift:** Continuous parameter updates repeatedly cause unrecoverable safety alignment degradation or cross-tenant leakage that cannot be contained by admission filtering or rollback.

### Reversal from Conditional Go to FULL GO (Unrestricted Scale-Up)
1. **Replicated Controller Pareto Dominance:** Multi-site independent replication (**F9**) confirms that a substrate-placement controller achieves statistically significant, Pareto-superior trajectory utility, calibration, and deletion closure over temporal RAG and periodic rebuilding at equal lifecycle cost.
2. **Certifiable Deletion & Rollback:** Development of an empirically verified deletion technique that guarantees derivative erasure and atomic bundle restoration under adaptive red-team attacks (**F11**).

### 1. Privacy Controls
* **Red-Team Assessment:** PII leakage occurs primarily through unindexed episodic memory and derivative text summaries.
* **Mandatory Control:** Direct raw user input must never be ingested directly into persistent parametric weights or shared retrieval indices. All persistent state must be assigned clear tenant, purpose, and valid-time metadata.

### 2. Poisoning Controls
* **Red-Team Assessment:** Low-fraction targeted poisoning ([AgentPoison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784); [PoisonedRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07867)) can corrupt retrieval and memory routing with minimal attacker footprint. Unlearning fails to clean poisoned weight updates ([Machine Unlearning Fails](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/7e810b2c75d69be186cadd2fe3febeab-Abstract-Conference.html)).
* **Mandatory Control:** Experience admission must act as the primary security barrier. Ingested state requires multi-source corroboration or sandboxed executable verification prior to promotion into shared serving layers.

### 3. Provenance Controls
* **Red-Team Assessment:** Provenance metadata ([C2PA 2.2](https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/index.html)) attests to record origin and cryptographic identity, but does **not** guarantee factual truth or safety.
* **Mandatory Control:** Use content-addressed dependency graphs to trace every derived embedding, summary, and adapter to its source event. Treat provenance as an auditing mechanism, not a factual verification oracle.

### 4. Deletion Controls
* **Red-Team Assessment:** "Complete unlearning" in neural weights is technically unverified. Deleting a row in a database leaves residual traces in embeddings, caches, and fine-tuned weights.
* **Mandatory Control:** Prohibit claims of "perfect" or "certified" unlearning. Issue **Qualified Deletion Receipts** that explicitly itemize: (a) removed source records, (b) rebuilt indices, (c) disabled routes, and (d) estimated residual risk based on membership-inference and behavioral probing.

### 5. Rollback Controls
* **Red-Team Assessment:** Registry alias switching (e.g., updating a model tag) creates partial rollback, leaving external memory, prompt caches, and retrieval indices out of sync.
* **Mandatory Control:** Rollback must execute as an atomic transaction across the **entire signed system bundle** (base weights, adapters, retrieval index snapshot, memory store state, router policy, and tool manifests).

### 6. Safety-Retention Controls
* **Red-Team Assessment:** Parameter updates routinely cause hidden regression in safety refusal boundaries, calibration, and grounding while task performance appears unaffected.
* **Mandatory Control:** Post-update safety qualification is non-negotiable. Every state modification invalidates prior safety certifications; updated bundles must pass shadow evaluation across safety, privacy, and tenant isolation benchmarks before serving release.
