## Q1 — Operational scope, taxonomy, demonstrated evidence

**Strongest current answer.** Two labels: **parametric continual learning** (durable mutation of weights or adapters) and **system-level continual adaptation** (durable mutation anywhere in the deployed system — retrieval indexes, episodic memory, persistent prompt state, skills, routers, tools, policy). Under either, a publishable learning claim requires an authorized, attributable state change, caused by an ordered experience stream, persisting beyond the current request, producing reproducible held-out improvement over matched reset/stateless and frozen full-context controls. Re-supplied request context is request-local adaptation. Every claim declares substrate and signed manifest, stream regime and identity assumptions, experience trust status, valid-time and transaction-time semantics, retention/transfer/recovery/reversibility class, compute and persistent-state budgets, and safety/privacy/deletion/rollback obligations. The strongest defensible empirical statement is **negative**: the literature shows bounded successes and diagnostic failures, and does not demonstrate an indefinitely learning frontier system jointly providing bounded resources, durable acquisition, safety retention, provenance, privacy, derivative-aware deletion, and coherent whole-bundle rollback.

**Primary URLs.** https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1551/ · https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/a774503daed55eb53c634847ae071ec7-Paper-Conference.pdf · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00368 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11020 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05661 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07734 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16003

**Retain.** The two-label taxonomy; the four-part durability test; the manifest list; the negative boundary claim in explicitly literature-scoped form.

**Narrow.** "The public evidence supports bounded successes" → *these 80 records, selected by undisclosed protocol*. The ledger has no stated inclusion criteria, no search strings, no recall estimate, no screened-but-excluded count. It is a curated bibliography, not a systematic review. This silently conditions all six answers and is the packet's largest uncorrected weakness.

**Downgrade.** Confidence 8/10 "on the operational definition and evidence boundary." A definition cannot carry empirical confidence — it can be useful or adopted, not 8/10 true. The boundary half is a claim about search completeness never specified. → 6. Also downgrade the Q1 source mix: three of five headline sources are 2026 preprints (one single-author), one ICLR paper with a synthetic principal diagnostic, one ACL paper at 1B/3B. Preprint survival rates are not priced anywhere.

**Delete.** Nothing. Q1's failures are calibration, not content.

**Hidden assumptions and category errors.** (i) *Assumed ledger completeness* — every "the literature does not show X" is "these 80 records do not show X"; absence in a curated set is weak evidence of absence, strength proportional to unmeasured recall. (ii) *Category error*: definitions scored as empirical claims; a definition's failure mode is unhelpfulness, not falsity. (iii) *Assumed decidability of "durable"* — under rolling KV caches, speculative prefill, session stitching, and agent loops, the request boundary is an implementation artifact; two engineers can classify the same system differently in good faith. (iv) *Assumed reconstructability of the reset counterfactual* — admitted unresolved, then made mandatory at Q5 and definitional at Q1; if unreconstructable in production, the definition is laboratory-only wearing deployment clothing. (v) *Causal-inference gap* — "caused by an ordered experience stream" is causal; the proposed test is associational, with no intervention required and no treatment of confounding by base-model updates, corpus refresh, traffic-mix shift, or evaluation drift.

**Counterargument.** The taxonomy is unfalsifiable scaffolding: it generates no predictions, forbids no observations, and mainly licenses reviewers to reject others' claims. Fields advance by building systems that learn, not by refining what counts as learning.

**Rebuttal.** Partly conceded — the taxonomy is not a hypothesis and should stop being scored as one. But it fails against this ledger's own record. Falling loss with zero free-form recall (2607.00368), coverage collapsing before accuracy (2604.23987), accuracy surviving while grounding fails (2607.02020), rank reversals across harnesses (2606.29914) are exactly what happens when a field builds without agreeing on the dependent variable. Fund it as instrumentation; do not publish it as a finding.

**Hardened answer.**
> We distinguish **parametric continual learning** from **system-level continual adaptation**. Both require, for a publishable learning claim: an authorized, attributable state change; causation by an ordered experience stream; persistence beyond the request boundary, **with that boundary explicitly defined for the system under test**; and reproducible held-out improvement over matched reset/stateless and frozen full-context controls. Re-supplied request context is request-local adaptation. Persistent retrieval, prompt state, or memory may qualify as system-level adaptation and must never be represented as equivalent to weight learning.
> **Evidence status.** Across 80 primary and official records — a curated set with unstated search protocol and unmeasured recall — we find bounded positive results and well-characterized diagnostic failures: replay and scheduling improve continual web pretraining at 1B/3B in one ACL 2025 protocol; some decline is rapidly recoverable in one ICLR 2025 protocol whose principal diagnostic is synthetic; lower update loss can coexist with zero free-form recall in one controlled nonce-fact diagnostic; sequential factual weight writes showed poor unprompted retention in one single-author, single-family preprint; persistent memory helped in limited domains while failing to dominate context or retrieval controls elsewhere.
> **We did not find, in this record set, a publicly documented frontier system jointly providing bounded resources, durable acquisition, safety retention, provenance, privacy, derivative-aware deletion, and coherent whole-bundle rollback. We make no claim about private systems and do not claim our search was exhaustive.**

**Confidence: 6/10.** Internal coherence and usefulness against documented measurement pathologies would alone be 8. The empirical half is weak: unspecified search protocol, three 2026 preprints in five headline slots, unresolved production counterfactual, unmeasured inter-rater reliability. I score the composite as published, not its best fragment. I am *not* uncertain about the two-label distinction itself, which should be adopted regardless.

**Recommendation: Conditional Go.** Adopt the taxonomy now as internal measurement discipline. Condition external publication of the boundary claim on published search protocol, completed reliability study, and a recall caveat at every instance.

**Decisive experiment.** *Taxonomy Applicability and Recall Audit.* Arm A: ≥5 independent blind raters classify 30 real deployed configurations (session-stitched agents, rolling-cache serving, persistent-memory products) under the published decision procedure. Arm B: a team that did not build the ledger runs a preregistered systematic search over the same window and reports missed records bearing on load-bearing claims.
**Stop condition.** Halt external publication of the boundary claim if Arm A yields Krippendorff's α < 0.70 on the parametric/system-level distinction, **or** Arm B surfaces ≥3 pre-cutoff primary records altering any Q1–Q6 load-bearing claim.

## Q2 — Plasticity, forgetting, interference control

**Strongest current answer.** No validated universal ordering exists among replay, importance regularization, orthogonal/sparse adapters, expansion, editing, dense tuning, and continual policy optimization. Operate plasticity as a constraint-keyed governed update service — immutable base checkpoint, quarantined candidate data, lawful provenance-bearing replay, revocable adapters or sparse regions, capacity/routing registry, release controller — selecting among no update, external state, modular update, sparse protected update, or offline dense consolidation according to stream and knowledge type, expected reuse, lawful retention obligations, interference and safety drift, capacity/routing/latency/serving cost, required attribution and reversibility, and total assurance and incident cost. Replay is the lawful-retention reference arm. Replay rate is not corpus size. Every parameter update suspends inherited safety and assurance claims until affected behaviors are re-qualified.

**Primary URLs.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01908 · https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.715/ · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24752 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05683 · https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1611835114 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05946 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15053 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04364

**Retain.** The refusal to rank — the most defensible position in the packet, and it should be foregrounded, not buried under an eight-factor procedure. Replay as reference arm. Replay-rate discipline. Post-update requalification.

**Narrow.** "Least irreversible adequate mechanism" is a safety preference, correctly labeled in the lineage section and stated in the bare imperative in the hardened answer. Move the qualifier inline.

**Downgrade.** The eight-factor selector to **untested engineering doctrine** — no study in the ledger evaluates a constraint-keyed selector against a fixed-mechanism policy. Elaborate, plausible, wholly unvalidated; 7/10 is unearned, → 4. Also downgrade the plasticity-loss position: the 5M–314M scope caveat (2606.24752) is stated correctly, then frontier plasticity loss is carried into the portfolio as a live design constraint. The packet wants it both ways.

**Delete.** The framing that a decision procedure answers the empirical question. Q2 asks which mechanisms control interference; the honest answer is "unknown, and here is why existing studies cannot tell us." Presenting the procedure as the hardened answer disguises a null result as a positive deliverable — the specific move this board exists to catch.

**Hidden assumptions and category errors.** (i) *Governance procedure substituted for empirical finding* — a skimming reader concludes the field has a mechanism-selection answer. (ii) *Assumed selector feasibility* — "total assurance and incident cost" and "expected reuse and acquisition difficulty" are not measurable at decision time; a selector keyed on unmeasurable inputs degrades to expert judgment with extra steps. (iii) *Assumed factor independence* — interference, capacity, routing cost, and reversibility are strongly coupled; eight separable keys assume an unsupported factorization. (iv) *Circular requalification scope* — for dense updates, the dependency analysis that would bound requalification is precisely what the update invalidates. (v) *Replication-language asymmetry* — the replay-free report (2605.15053) is correctly called unreplicated and bounded; the symmetric statement that the replay evidence is also protocol-bounded (one 2025 preprint, five languages, 100B tokens each) is missing. The asymmetry is a tell.

**Counterargument.** Refusing to rank is decision paralysis dressed as rigor. Practitioners ship Monday. The evidence leans: replay works broadly and has since 2018 (https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.11682); orthogonal and sparse methods reduce interference on every finite sequence tested; editors degrade over long sequences (2503.05683). "No validated ordering" is true and useless; a board that only forbids is not doing its job.

**Rebuttal.** Right that the deliverable must be actionable; wrong about what the evidence licenses. The answer is neither to rank nor to hide behind an unvalidated selector, but to state the **defensible leans with exact scope attached** — which the ledger supports and the hardened answer omits. Replay is the best *reference* arm for breadth of protocol coverage, not for winning matched comparisons that were never run. Editor degradation on evaluated long sequences forbids unbounded edit chains without declaring editing useless. Sparse/orthogonal finite-protocol support justifies preferring them over dense updates where reversibility matters, on explicitly safety grounds. That is a shippable stack with honest error bars, strictly more useful than a selector keyed on unmeasurable inputs.

**Hardened answer.**
> **No validated universal ordering exists.** The studies that would establish one do not share protocols, model families, stream types, compute, or persistent-state budgets, and no matched-resource tournament has been run. This is a null result about the state of evidence and we report it as one.
> **Scoped leans.** Replay has the broadest protocol coverage and serves as the lawful-retention reference arm — not because it wins matched comparisons, which have not been run. Tested editors degrade over long sequences in the WikiBigEdit protocol, forbidding unbounded edit chains for those editors, and not establishing that all future editing methods must degrade. Orthogonal and sparse methods reduce interference on evaluated finite academic sequences and do not establish indefinite capacity. Importance regularization has one narrow positive LLM result at one language pair. Continual policy optimization has one Qwen3-VL-8B result. Plasticity loss is demonstrated at 5M–314M non-embedding parameters with sublinear onset scaling; **frontier extrapolation is unverified and we do not assume it.**
> **Operating policy, explicitly safety-motivated rather than empirically validated.** Prefer the least irreversible adequate mechanism. Treat every parameter update as suspending inherited safety and assurance claims until utility, calibration, grounding, privacy, tool-policy, tenant-isolation, and deletion behaviors are re-qualified. Keep dense consolidation offline, rebuildable, lineage-complete, and covered by atomic whole-system rollback. **We do not claim these policies are experimentally validated; we claim they are correct posture under acknowledged ignorance.**

**Confidence: 4/10.** The null result itself merits ~9 — protocol incompatibility is documented and barely contestable. The *published answer*, whose operational core is the selector, merits 4: nothing tests it, several inputs are unmeasurable, factor independence is unsupported. The controller's 7 scores a procedure with zero evaluating studies.

**Recommendation: Conditional Go.** Go on publishing the null result and scoped leans. No-Go on presenting the selector as a conclusion until it is a preregistered tournament arm; it may be described as working policy with the disclaimer attached.

**Decisive experiment.** *Matched-Resource Mechanism Tournament with a Selector Arm.* One fixed mixed stream (facts, procedures, preferences). Arms: replay, importance regularization, orthogonal/sparse adapters, expansion, editing, dense tuning, periodic retraining, **and the selector as its own arm**. Matched compute, inference budget, persistent bytes; randomized orders; ≥2 model families; ≥2 independent harnesses; preregistered protocol, predicted ordering, and analysis plan. Report backward transfer, coverage, calibration, grounding, safety retention, and full lifecycle cost including routing, retirement, evaluation, incidents.
**Stop condition.** Abandon the selector as a deliverable if it fails to beat the best single fixed mechanism on the preregistered composite in ≥2 of 3 stream types, **or** if its advantage does not survive the second family and second harness. Separately, abandon the refusal-to-rank position if one stable ordering reproduces across both families, both harnesses, replay-lawful and replay-prohibited regimes, and all randomized orders.

## Q3 — Memory, retrieval, adapters, experts, editing, consolidation

**Strongest current answer.** Heterogeneous state placement is a **falsifiable risk-control hypothesis**, not an established architecture: transient evidence in request/session context; mutable facts in versioned temporal retrieval; tenant experience in isolated episodic memory; recurring procedures in signed skills or revocable adapters; repeatedly validated capabilities in sparse modular state; broad base-weight consolidation rarely and offline. Placement follows mutability, attribution, reuse, latency, lifecycle cost, required reversibility. Direct comparative evidence covers request context as control, trusted versioned temporal retrieval against several tested editors, and one single-domain persistent-feedback memory system. General episodic memory, signed skills/adapters, sparse modules, and broad consolidation are **unpriced tiers**. External memory relocates interference rather than eliminating it. Deleting a source row does not establish closure when derivatives exist.

**Primary URLs.** https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.546/ · https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27003 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05661 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22157 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27773 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07392 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29914

**Retain.** The unpriced-tier finding — the sharpest single observation in the packet. Interference relocation. Derivative-deletion incompleteness. The Q2/Q3 weakest-link reconciliation.

**Narrow.** "Trusted versioned temporal retrieval": the supporting result (2026.findings-acl.546) requires a *trusted* temporal KB. In deployment the KB is populated from the same untrusted stream Q4 spends its length quarantining. The evidence supports temporal retrieval *given a trusted KB*, not *as a way to handle untrusted streams*. The packet crosses this line unmarked — substantive, not stylistic.

**Downgrade.** The six-tier stack from "minimum testable hypothesis" to what it is: **two placements with scoped support plus four candidate tiers with no comparative evidence**. "Six-tier hierarchy" as a noun phrase does unsupported rhetorical work. Also downgrade Pulse 3's "provisional control and engineering default for mutable facts": the caveat is correct, but an engineering default in a leadership pulse becomes standing architecture within one planning cycle — the exact lock-in Claude's Round-2 dissent identified, recorded and then partly defeated by the document's own recommendation.

**Delete.** Any reliance on ChronoMem (2607.27773) beyond "rollback machinery has been implemented" — a preprint dated **one day before cutoff** with author-asserted priority. Headline placement of a one-day-old source is a calibration failure independent of its merit.

**Hidden assumptions and category errors.** (i) *Assumed tier separability* — real knowledge is simultaneously transient, mutable-factual, tenant-experiential, and procedural (a changed customer address is all four); subtractive ablation on non-separable tiers is uninterpretable. (ii) *Category error*: a risk-control hypothesis evaluated by a utility experiment — the hierarchy is justified on reversibility, attribution, and deletion; F1 measures trajectory utility and assurance; a tier can correctly lose on utility while being exactly right for deletion closure. The packet does not notice this. (iii) *Assumed trusted KB*, as above. (iv) *Confounded promotion* — reuse frequency is confounded with retrieval ranking: highly ranked items get retrieved, reused, and promoted independent of value; the criterion manufactures its own evidence. (v) *Single-result generalization* — the feedback-memory result (2607.22157) is single-author, single-domain, four trials, 2026-07-24, correctly refused for generalization and then given headline placement at Q3 and Q6, which implies the weight the caveat retracts.

**Counterargument.** The unpriced-tier critique proves too much. Every architecture contains components never ablated against their own absence; nobody A/B tests the existence of a cache tier. Demanding per-tier marginal-value evidence before construction is a standard no production system has met — isolated demand for rigor.

**Rebuttal.** Not isolated, because the *justification* is not ordinary. A cache tier is justified by latency and cost, verifiable in an afternoon. These tiers are justified by **deletion closure, attribution, reversibility, and safety retention** — properties unobservable without deliberate adversarial testing, which regulators will be asked to accept, and which the packet says are not established. When the warrant is a safety and compliance claim, per-component evidence is the ordinary standard. And the counterargument undercuts itself: tiers justified on ordinary engineering grounds would not need five contenders and eighty citations.

**Hardened answer.**
> Heterogeneous state placement is a **falsifiable risk-control hypothesis**, not a validated hierarchy.
> **Placements with direct comparative support:** request/session context, which functions as a control and which one benchmark found competitive with or superior to dedicated agent memory; and versioned temporal retrieval for mutable facts, which outperformed several tested editors **in a setting presupposing a trusted temporal knowledge base — a premise that does not hold for untrusted deployment streams.**
> **Candidate tiers with no evidence against their own absence:** general episodic memory, signed skills and revocable adapters, sparse modular state, broad consolidation. One single-author, single-domain, four-trial preprint reports feedback memory beating static RAG; this does not validate episodic memory as a general tier. **These four are unpriced and must not enter standing architecture before subtractive evaluation.**
> **Established negatives.** External memory relocates interference: retrieval misses, stale and conflicting records, poisoning, tenant leakage, context competition, routing failure, derivative proliferation. Deleting a source row does not establish closure when embeddings, summaries, caches, traces, replay corpora, adapters, router policies, or consolidated weights derive from it. Demonstrated semantic-rollback machinery is not derivative deletion.
> **Because these tiers are justified on reversibility, attribution, and deletion grounds rather than utility, their evaluation must include those endpoints. A tier losing on utility may still be required for deletion closure, and the converse.**

**Confidence: 4/10.** The negative claims merit ~8 and are Q3's real contribution. The hierarchy *as a testable object* merits 4: non-separability may make it untestable by the proposed method, endpoints are mismatched to the justification, and the strongest support carries a trusted-KB precondition deployment violates. I go below the controller's integrated figure because the endpoint mismatch is unacknowledged anywhere and undermines the experiment the programme turns on.

**Recommendation: Conditional Go.** Go on F1 **only after** amendment: deletion/attribution/revocation endpoints, cross-tier overlap measurement, untrusted-stream arm. No-Go on any architecture posture or engineering default naming the hierarchy or an unpriced tier until F1 returns. Amend Pulse 3's posture line before sealing.

**Decisive experiment.** *Amended F1 Subtractive Ablation Ladder.* Single site, minimum instrumented hierarchy on one mixed stream. Measure and report cross-tier content overlap first. Remove tiers singly and in pairs against three comparison arms: temporal RAG with trusted KB, temporal RAG with untrusted stream-populated KB, governed periodic rebuilding. Matched inference budget and persistent bytes. Predeclare the load-bearing tier. Four independent endpoint families: trajectory utility; assurance and safety retention; deletion coverage and derivative closure; attribution completeness and revocation-drill pass rate.
**Stop condition.** Delete a tier only if removal produces no reproducible loss across **all four** families and its overlap is below the preregistered threshold. Cancel the multi-site programme if temporal RAG or governed periodic rebuilding matches the full hierarchy on all four at lower lifecycle cost. Declare F1 invalid and redesign before concluding anything if measured overlap exceeds threshold for a majority of tier pairs.

## Q4 — Admission, governance, privacy, provenance, deletion, rollback

**Strongest current answer.** Deployment experience is untrusted evidence, not training data by default. Every durable mutation passes: capture (event, outcome, actors, tools, source hashes, subject identity, valid time, consent scope, legal basis); classify (tenant, purpose, sensitivity, volatility, retention, sharing); quarantine and screen (poison, conflict, duplication, privacy exposure, unverifiable feedback); require corroboration or executable verification before shared promotion; shadow-test (utility, calibration, grounding, safety, privacy, tenant isolation); select the least irreversible adequate substrate; release a signed complete-system manifest through staged deployment; preserve lineage, monitoring, rollback, qualified deletion receipts. Provenance attests asserted origin and history — not truth, consent, authorization, or harmlessness. Deletion closure is a testable coverage gate under a documented residual-risk standard. Rollback means coherent restoration of the complete deployable bundle.

**Primary URLs.** https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/7e810b2c75d69be186cadd2fe3febeab-Abstract-Conference.html · https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07867 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05012 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04379 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27080 · https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/index.html · https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/art_17/oj/eng · https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/general-purpose-ai-obligations-under-ai-act · https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

**Retain.** Untrusted-by-default experience; the eight-step skeleton; the provenance-is-not-truth distinction, stated with unusual precision; refusal of "perfect deletion"; whole-bundle rollback. The packet's strongest question.

**Narrow.** "Testable coverage gate" → tested coverage against *enumerable* derivative classes. Whether the derivative graph is completely enumerable at production scale is unestablished; an unenumerable graph makes coverage unmeasurable, not merely incomplete. Also narrow screening efficacy: all poisoning evidence in the ledger is **non-adaptive** (2607.27080 scopes explicitly; 2407.12784 and 2402.07867 use targeted threat models). There is **zero evidence** about screening against an adaptive attacker who knows the rule, yet the pipeline treats screening as a functioning control.

**Downgrade.** Deletion receipts. A receipt asserting what was removed, rebuilt, disabled, made inaccessible, or retained as residual risk is a legal instrument with untested standing — no regulator has accepted one, no court evaluated one, and Article 17 compliance is not established by self-issued attestation.

**Delete.** Any implication the pipeline is deployable as specified. Per-event consent scope and legal basis, complete derivative lineage, and six-family shadow testing of every mutation have no cost model. Whether governed admission justifies its overhead sits in the unresolved list while the pipeline is stated as a requirement.

**Hidden assumptions and category errors.** (i) *Assumed adaptive-attacker robustness with no supporting evidence* — the largest red-team gap in the packet. (ii) *Category error*: technical coverage as legal compliance — Article 17 erasure involves controller/processor roles, exceptions, proportionality, and supervisory interpretation; the synthesis rejects interchangeability, then builds receipts whose purpose is to discharge a legal obligation via technical measurement. (iii) *Assumed derivative-graph enumerability*. (iv) *Category error*: provenance as admission control — a perfectly provenanced poisoned record passes by construction; stated correctly at claim level, violated at pipeline level. (v) *Unpriced governance overhead*, unresolved yet mandatory. (vi) *Causal-inference gap in poison detection* — screening detects by pattern; an adaptive attacker optimizes against the detector; the evidence cannot distinguish "screening works" from "screening works against attackers who did not try to evade it."

**Counterargument.** The regime is so heavy it forecloses what it governs. If every mutation requires consent capture, quarantine, corroboration, six-family shadow testing, signed manifests, lineage, and rollback drills, organizations will run periodic retraining from a curated corpus instead — obtaining deletion and rollback free by construction. The regime's conclusion is that continual learning should not be built. That is Claude's own Round-2 kill condition.

**Rebuttal.** The strongest counterargument in this report, and one the board should **adopt** rather than rebut. If governed rebuilding obtains closure and rollback by construction and matches on utility, continual learning reduces to corpus curation and retraining cadence, and the right move is to say so and stop. The regime is a **cost-revealing device**: it makes the price of durable mutation explicit so the comparison is honest. What must change is framing — the pipeline is the price of durable mutation, with the explicit corollary that **the price may exceed the value**. That corollary is missing and is the most important thing Q4 could say.

**Hardened answer.**
> Deployment experience is **untrusted evidence, not training data by default**. The admission and release pipeline is the governance atom for durable mutation: capture with legal basis and valid time; classify; quarantine and screen; require corroboration or executable verification before shared promotion; shadow-test utility, calibration, grounding, safety, privacy, tenant isolation; select the least irreversible adequate substrate; release a signed complete-system manifest through staged deployment; preserve lineage, monitoring, rollback, and qualified receipts.
> **Evidenced limits.** Poison effects can survive unlearning under tested methods and attacks. Small poison fractions can corrupt agent memory and retrieval pipelines. **All poisoning evidence in our record set is non-adaptive: we have no evidence about screening against an attacker who knows the rule, and we claim none.** Provenance attests origin and history and does not establish truth, consent, authorization, or harmlessness.
> **Deletion closure is a testable coverage gate over enumerable derivative classes under a documented residual-risk standard — not achieved, certifiable, or legally dispositive.** Whether the derivative graph is enumerable at production scale is unestablished. **Receipts are engineering artifacts with no established regulatory or evidentiary standing; technical residual-influence testing does not determine legal erasure under GDPR Article 17.**
> **Cost corollary, stated as prominently as the requirements.** The full cost of this regime is unmeasured and may exceed the value of durable mutation. Governed periodic rebuilding obtains deletion closure and rollback by construction. **If rebuilding matches governed mutation on utility at lower lifecycle cost, the correct conclusion is that continual learning reduces to corpus curation and retraining cadence.** This is a live possibility we are testing, not a rhetorical concession.

**Confidence: 6/10.** The negative and distinction-drawing claims merit ~8 and are the best epistemic work in the packet. The pipeline *as a working control system* merits 4: no adaptive-attacker evidence, unestablished enumerability, no receipt standing, unmeasured cost.

**Recommendation: Conditional Go.** Go on the framework as design guidance and the distinctions as publishable findings. No-Go on representing screening as validated, on external receipt use pending counsel, and on mandating the pipeline before a per-event cost model exists.

**Decisive experiment.** *Adaptive Red-Team and Deletion-Coverage Drill.* Arm A: an independent red team with full white-box knowledge of screening rules, thresholds, corroboration requirements, and shadow-test criteria attempts durable poisoned placement; measure admission rate, time-to-durable-state, derivative propagation, and survival through deletion. Arm B: seed known targets across every enumerated derivative class, execute deletion, then attempt recovery through each class independently — embeddings, summaries, caches, traces, replay corpora, adapters, router policies, consolidated weights. Both report governance cost per event.
**Stop condition.** Halt production admission if the adaptive team achieves durable placement at ≥10× the non-adaptive rate. Void completed receipts and suspend issuance if Arm B recovers or activates a deleted target through any derivative class. Abandon governed durable mutation for periodic rebuilding if per-event governance cost exceeds the preregistered ceiling.

## Q5 — Longitudinal evaluation, safety retention, assurance

**Strongest current answer.** Evaluate a trajectory of complete system manifests, not a final checkpoint. Test prequentially before each event is learned and retrospectively after each stage, because endpoint accuracy cannot distinguish acquisition failure, destructive interference, recoverable access loss, retrieval failure, grounding drift, calibration collapse, policy refusal, or plasticity loss. Measure acquisition and transfer; degradation, recoverability, recovery cost; fixed-budget plasticity; calibration, coverage, abstention, grounding; temporal consistency; safety, privacy, tool-policy, tenant-isolation regressions; routing, memory conflict, poison persistence; state growth, compute, latency, energy, human review; deletion and rollback drills; rejected releases and incidents. Govern the list by a preregistered **metric-retention rule** — retain a metric only where it reproducibly detects a distinct seeded failure or changes a decision a simpler protocol misses — and publish negative ablations. Reset/stateless, frozen full-context, strong temporal RAG, and periodic retraining are mandatory controls. Safety, privacy, tenant isolation, and deletion are non-compensable gates.

## Red-Team Review of Controls

**Privacy.** *Specified:* per-event consent scope and legal basis, tenant classification, isolation testing, privacy regression in shadow tests. *Gaps:* consent-scope capture has no production-volume cost model; tenant isolation is tested with no isolation-breach threat model in the packet; **evaluation logs and replay corpora are named governed state with no retention schedule and are among the most privacy-dense artifacts the system will hold**; the derivative graph privacy obligations must follow is of unestablished enumerability. *Verdict:* **Under-specified** — the design assumes an enumerable graph and unmeasured cost, neither established.

**Poisoning.** *Specified:* quarantine, screening, corroboration, executable verification, shadow testing, poison-persistence monitoring. *Gaps:* **every poisoning result in the ledger is non-adaptive** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27080 scopes explicitly; https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12784, https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07867, and https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04379 use targeted threat models. Corroboration is defeatable by an attacker controlling volume within one channel. Poison survival through unlearning is *demonstrated* (ICLR 2025), so the failure is durable once landed. *Verdict:* **The largest red-team gap in the packet.** All current defensive figures are upper bounds and must be labeled so wherever they appear.

**Provenance.** *Specified:* C2PA 2.2 bindings (https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/index.html), source hashes, actor and tool capture, signed manifests, dependency lineage. *Gaps:* the packet states provenance is not truth, then uses it at intake as if it filtered risk. **A perfectly provenanced poisoned record passes every provenance check by construction.** Manifest signing covers what was deployed, not whether it should have been. *Verdict:* **Correct in principle, misapplied in the pipeline.** Provenance is audit and attribution capability, not admission control, and must stop being positioned adjacent to one.

**Deletion.** *Specified:* coverage gate over source records, embeddings, indexes, summaries, caches, logs, replay corpora, adapters, router policies, consolidated weights; qualified receipts stating removed, rebuilt, disabled, inaccessible, or residual. *Gaps:* production-scale enumerability of the derivative graph is unestablished, and an unenumerable graph makes coverage unmeasurable rather than merely partial; receipts have no regulatory or evidentiary standing; behavioral unavailability is correctly distinguished from causal erasure, but **no validated causal-erasure probe for LLMs exists in this ledger** — the closest source (https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05012) is a 2019 linear/statistical-model result. *Verdict:* **Honest about its limits and not yet a control.** The "testable coverage gate" framing is right; the testing has not been done.

**Rollback.** *Specified:* coherent restoration of the complete deployable bundle; semantic-memory rollback and registry aliases explicitly demoted to components. *Gaps:* the only implementation evidence is a preprint dated one day before cutoff (https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27773) with author-asserted priority; **no rollback drill result appears anywhere in the ledger**; and rollback interacts unresolved with deletion — rolling back to a pre-deletion state may restore deleted data, an interaction **nothing in the packet addresses**, and a concrete, likely operational failure. *Verdict:* **Defined but undemonstrated, with one unaddressed interaction that should be treated as a priority defect.**

**Safety retention.** *Specified:* every parameter update suspends inherited safety claims until requalification; safety, privacy, tenant isolation, and deletion are non-compensable gates; supported by layer-selective safe merging (https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1761/) and gradient-based sample selection (https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.942/). *Gaps:* requalification scope "follows dependency and risk analysis," circular for dense updates; the two supporting papers cover four models/tasks and one filtering method respectively and establish no general retention guarantee; **the non-compensable gate is the strongest control in the packet and depends entirely on the Q5 metric set, which does not exist.** *Verdict:* **Correct policy, sound instinct, currently unmeasurable.** The gate cannot bind until the retained set is defined.

**Cross-cutting.** The controls are well-conceived and consistently confuse *specification* with *validation*. Not one has been tested end-to-end under adversarial conditions. The governance language — pipelines, gates, receipts, manifests — has the texture of an operating system in service; it is a design document. That gap is where a real adversary would work, and closing it should precede any further funding.

**Go now:** the Q5 divergence finding and trajectory-evaluation principle; the Q1 two-label taxonomy as internal measurement discipline; the Q2 null result with scoped leans; the Q4 distinctions on provenance, deletion, and rollback; the amended stage-1 gate and stage-2 reference infrastructure.

Without all four, stage 1 cannot answer the question the programme is sequenced around, and a decisive-looking result would be worse than none.

**Closing calibration note.** My six scores run below the controller's on every question — one systematic difference, not six disagreements: the controller scored each answer's strongest defensible fragment; I scored each as a reader receives it. Both are legitimate, they must not be mixed in one table, and the packet currently mixes them. The finding this board most wants recorded: **the packet's epistemic discipline is excellent at the claim level and degrades at the document level.** Prohibitions are stated correctly and then violated by the grammar of the sections that follow — most visibly in Pulse 3, where "convergence" is a section heading directly above a prohibition on the word. Hedges do not survive summarization; sentence structure does. The fix is not more caveats: write the load-bearing sentences so that removing the caveat leaves a sentence that is still true.
