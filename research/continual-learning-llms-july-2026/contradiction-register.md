## Whitepaper-critical corrections

1. **Do not use “forgetting” as a single variable.** Separate destructive knowledge loss, task/readout misalignment, transient stability gap, grounding drift, and plasticity loss.
2. **Do not present 2.6× as general capability parity.** It is comparable held-out Common-Crawl loss in TiC-LM’s protocol.
3. **Do not claim O-LoRA generically achieves near-zero BWT.** Attribute near-zero forgetting only to the specific Latent-LoRA author-reported experiments, with finite-benchmark caveats.
4. **Remove Antigravity’s 35%–68% degradation range** unless an exact primary table is supplied.
5. **Treat replay as the mandatory strong baseline, not the universal winner.**
6. **Treat the hybrid hierarchy as a research/design recommendation**, not a demonstrated or independently supported architecture.
7. **Use source-status discipline:** preprint, workshop, proceedings paper, first-party product claim, and independently reproduced result are not equivalent.
8. **Keep CL-Bench, context learning, temporal QA, RAG freshness, knowledge editing, and sequential weight learning as separate evaluation objects.**
