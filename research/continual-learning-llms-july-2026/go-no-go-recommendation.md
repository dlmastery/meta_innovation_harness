# Go/No-Go Recommendation

**Decision:** **CONDITIONAL GO** for measurement, governance, and one bounded falsification gate. **NO-GO** for committing to the heterogeneous hierarchy, open-ended online shared-weight mutation, or consolidation infrastructure.

## Authorize now

1. Trajectory-native evaluation with reset/stateless, frozen full-context, strong temporal-RAG, and governed periodic-rebuild controls.
2. Complete signed system manifests, resource accounting, governed admission, derivative lineage, qualified deletion receipts, tenant-isolation tests, and coherent whole-bundle rollback.
3. One preregistered, single-site subtractive ablation under absolute engineering, wall-time, lifecycle-spend, inference, persistent-state, and optimization ceilings.

## Do not authorize now

- Multi-site hierarchy deployment.
- Autonomous promotion into shared weights.
- Claims of deletion closure or production maturity.
- Architecture funding based on five shared-pack contender agreement.
- Mechanism rankings assembled from protocol-incompatible studies.

## Advancement gate

Advance only a tier that shows reproducible marginal trajectory value over its own absence and over strong fixed-substrate controls, while every non-compensable safety, privacy, poisoning, deletion, tenant-isolation, rollback, and lifecycle-cost gate passes. Same-team second-family or second-harness runs are robustness checks; an independent team or site must reproduce load-bearing recent claims before they determine architecture funding.

## Kill condition

Cancel hierarchy and consolidation investment if a simpler fixed substrate repeatedly matches the controller on utility, assurance, reversibility, and total lifecycle cost; if removing a tier causes no reproducible loss; if poison reaches serving; or if independent reproduction fails.
