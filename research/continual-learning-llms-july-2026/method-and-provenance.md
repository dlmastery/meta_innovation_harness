## Independence limitation

The five contenders are heterogeneous synthesis participants, not independent empirical replications. They received overlapping controller context and peer-derived material. Repeated conclusions therefore indicate synthesis consistency only.

## Evidence policy

Primary and official locators are canonical. Preprints, work-in-progress reports, proceedings papers, vendor/repository claims, theory, inference, and speculation retain separate status. Exact numerical claims must preserve model, stream, baseline, metric, protocol, and scope. HTTP 403 is treated as access restriction rather than proof of a dead link.

## Corpus construction and screening

The corpus was assembled for a decision-focused research battle rather than a registered systematic review. Five contenders independently searched the public web under a common six-question brief and a hard cutoff of **2026-07-31 23:59 UTC**. The controller then extracted direct primary or official locators from their dossiers, resolved exact title/URL identity, deduplicated sources, reconciled source deltas, and admitted records only when the source was accessible or independently identity-verifiable and its supported claim and caveat could be stated. Secondary summaries were retained only as discovery aids or clearly labeled context; they do not carry load-bearing empirical claims.

Screening prioritized work that directly bears on continual pretraining, sequential factual learning/editing, memory and retrieval, plasticity/interference, longitudinal evaluation, poisoning, provenance, privacy/deletion, rollback, lifecycle cost, and programme governance. The controller also admitted disconfirming results and official legal or technical materials needed to bound claims. Source identity, status, date/cutoff, quantitative scope, and title/URL pairing were audited before claim synthesis. The resulting ledger has **80 canonical records**.

This process did **not** preregister databases, search strings, language limits, inclusion/exclusion criteria, or duplicate-screening adjudication; it does not report screened-but-excluded counts or a recall estimate. The corpus is therefore curated and decision-focused, not exhaustive. Absence claims are limited to publicly documented systems in these 80 reviewed records and do not cover private deployments or sources the searches missed.

## Operational edge cases and control substitutions

- The request boundary must be declared before testing. Session stitching, persistent caches, summaries, tool state, and hidden server-side memory count as durable system state when they survive the declared boundary; request-local context that disappears under a verified reset does not.
- A reset/stateless control requires a frozen reference environment and a manifest showing that model, adapters, indexes, memories, prompts, tools, router, caches, and policy returned to the baseline state. If a true reset is unavailable, the substitute must be named, benchmarked against a true reset in a laboratory setting where possible, and treated as a confound rather than equivalent evidence.
- Every experiment must disclose caching, contamination, prompt or data leakage, environment drift, control substitutions, unavailable controls, and state that cannot be inspected or reset.
- Persistent evaluation logs are governed state: they require access, contamination, retention, and deletion controls and may not silently enter later training or evaluation.

The canonical ledger contains 80 records. At the final staging audit, every external URL used in the publication tree mapped exactly to one of those canonical records; the access-aware link checker reported zero failed links and one access-restricted publisher endpoint. Secret scanning reports counts and paths only and never prints matched values.

## Known provenance limitations

- Grok CLI model provenance remains ambiguous: its model listing reported an unauthenticated state and listed `grok-4.5`, while a substantive execution identified `grok-4.5-build`. The run records the observed execution label without claiming stronger provider provenance.
- Contender isolation establishes bounded input exposure, not epistemic independence. Shared source selection and controller framing can create correlated omissions.
- The 80-record corpus is curated rather than a registered systematic review; absence claims are limited to the reviewed public record set.
- Same-team second-family or second-harness checks are robustness tests. Independent-team or independent-site reproduction is separately required before architecture or funding commitment.
